### 1. `region_visualization.ipynb` — time-encoded trajectory plot (built earlier, refined today)
Interactive plotly view of `LLM_generated_region_experiment_v1/v2` trajectories projected onto the
2D plane (axis 1 = `w*`, axis 2 = `sum_i w_tilde_i`). Key features, all in
`plot_trajectories_with_time(...)`:
- **Time encoded by tick density** — equal-step markers; where GD spends many steps (the slow,
  late-time regime, since `||w_t|| ~ log t`) the ticks pile up. Hover shows the exact step number.
- Dropped the earlier `log10(step)` viridis colorbar (redundant with tick density, and it overlapped
  the legend). Each **trajectory is now one color, matched to its `w_tilde_i`** ray.
- **`w*`** drawn as a gold diamond (its x-coord is exactly `||w*||`, so you can compare magnitudes).
- **`w_tilde_i`** drawn as a color-coded dashed ray from the origin (the asymptote each trajectory
  should converge toward) plus an `x` marker at the actual (finite, margin-1) `w_tilde_i` point.
- **V2 stop markers**: green square = early-stop iterate, purple star = test-loss argmin. Each is a
  separate legend entry so they toggle independently.
