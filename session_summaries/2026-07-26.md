## Key design (discussed at length before coding)
- **Store per-axis dot products, not full trajectories.** For each iterate store
  `P[i,t,a] = ⟨w_{i,t}, v_a⟩` against unit candidate axes `v_a`, plus the axis Gram matrix
  `G[a,b] = ⟨v_a, v_b⟩`. This is ~200× smaller than the d-dim trajectory (`T×n_axes` vs `T×d`).
- **View-time reconstruction.** Any chosen axis pair `(a=x, b=y)` is turned into an orthonormal
  2D frame at draw time via Gram–Schmidt on `G`:
  `x = P[·,a]/√G[a,a]`, `y = (P[·,b] − (G[a,b]/G[a,a])P[·,a]) / √(G[b,b] − G[a,b]²/G[a,a])`.
  Derived/checked together (incl. the `√(1 − cos²)` denominator, which must stay even for unit axes).
- **Magnitude is preserved.** Axes are unit-normalized *for projection only*; the raw `w*` and
  `w̃_i` are returned separately at true magnitude so markers land at real scale
  (`w*` at `(‖w*‖, 0)` when x-axis = `w*`). Verified.
- **Anchor asymmetry** is inherent: whichever vector is chosen for x sits exactly on +x; the
  y-pick is orthogonalized (`v₂^⊥`) and tilts by its correlation with x.
