## Paper Reading: GCV Formula (Eq. 5)

Read "Failures and Successes of Cross-Validation for Early-Stopped Gradient Descent" (Patil, Wu, Tibshirani, AISTATS 2024).

**Eq. 5 — GCV estimator:**

$$\hat{R}^{gcv}(\hat{\beta}_k) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{(y_i - x_i^\top \hat{\beta}_k)^2}{(1 - \text{tr}[H_k]/n)^2}$$

- The numerator is the **training residuals** squared (average over samples).
- `tr` is the **trace** of the hat matrix $H_k$ (not training error) — it measures effective degrees of freedom of GD at step k.
- The denominator corrects for the downward bias of training error (optimism correction). As k grows and GD interpolates, `tr[H_k]/n → 1` and GCV → ∞ — this is the inconsistency the paper proves.
- Main result: GCV is inconsistent in the overparameterized regime; LOOCV is consistent.

## GCV Implementation

### Key idea for tr[H_k]

GD iterates are linear smoothers with hat matrix $H_k$. Its eigenvalues in the column space of X are $1 - (1-\delta\lambda_j)^k$, where $\lambda_j$ are eigenvalues of $X^\top X/n$. So:

$$\text{tr}[H_k] = \sum_j \left[1 - (1 - \delta\lambda_j)^k\right]$$

This is computed cheaply: precompute eigenvalues once (using the smaller of $XX^\top/n$ or $X^\top X/n$), then one vectorized line per iteration.

### Functions added to the class (by Ali, into the notebook)

- **`run_GCV(X_train, y_train, eta, max_T, ...)`**: precomputes eigenvalues of gram matrix once, then runs GD loop tracking `gcv_k = evaluate(w, X_train, y_train) / (1 - tr[H_k]/n)^2` at each step k.
- **`generate_samples_and_run_GCV(n, p, ...)`**: wrapper that generates data and calls `run_GCV`, parallel to the existing `generate_samples_and_run_LOOCV`.
